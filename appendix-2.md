# Appendix 2 – Occupational Exposure Flowchart

[ EXPOSURE EVENT OCCURS ]
│
├─► Percutaneous Injury (Needle-stick / Sharps)
├─► Mucous Membrane Splash (Eyes, Nose, Mouth)
└─► Non-Intact Skin Exposure (Broken skin / Abrasion)
│
▼
[ IMMEDIATE FIRST AID ]
│
├─► Percutaneous / Skin: Wash gently with soap and warm water.
│   DO NOT scrub, squeeze, or deliberately force bleeding.
└─► Mucous Membrane: Flush immediately with large volumes of clean water or sterile saline.
│
▼
[ NOTIFY SUPERVISOR ]
│
▼
[ SEEK URGENT MEDICAL EVALUATION ]
│
└─► Attending medical professional determines required blood tests, baseline screening,
and post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP) per clinical guidelines.
│
▼
[ DOCUMENT INCIDENT ]
│
└─► Complete factual, objective workplace incident report form.
