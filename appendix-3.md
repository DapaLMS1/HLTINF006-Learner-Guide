# Appendix 3 – Dental Instrument Reprocessing Workflow

[ 1. POINT-OF-USE CARE ]
Wipe heavy debris chairside; keep instruments moist.
│
▼
[ 2. SAFE TRANSPORT ]
Transfer in rigid, puncture-resistant, leak-proof, covered container.
│
▼
[ 3. CLEANING ]
Automated washer-disinfector, ultrasonic cleaner, or manual wash.
│
▼
[ 4. RINSING & DRYING ]
Rinse detergent residues; dry completely.
│
▼
[ 5. INSPECTION ]
Inspect under good lighting for cleanliness and damage.
│
▼
[ 6. PACKAGING ]
Package items requiring sterilisation with process indicators.
│
▼
[ 7. STERILISATION ]
Steam steriliser cycle per manufacturer instructions and workplace policy.
│
▼
[ 8. STORAGE & TRACKING ]
Store in clean, dry environment; record load details in logbook.
