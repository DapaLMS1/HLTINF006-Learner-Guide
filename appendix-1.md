# Appendix 1 – IPC Quick Reference Sheet

* **Standard Precautions:** Applied universally to every patient care interaction, regardless of suspected or confirmed infection status.
* **Hand Hygiene Moments:** 
  1. Before touching a patient
  2. Before a clean/aseptic procedure
  3. After body fluid exposure risk
  4. After touching a patient
  5. After touching patient surroundings
* **Hand Hygiene Methods:** Alcohol-Based Hand Rub (ABHR) for routine clean hands; liquid soap and water when hands are visibly soiled or contaminated with organic matter.
* **PPE Sequence (Donning):** Gown/Apron \(\rightarrow\) Mask/Respirator \(\rightarrow\) Eyewear/Face Shield \(\rightarrow\) Gloves.
* **PPE Sequence (Removal):** Gloves \(\rightarrow\) Eyewear/Face Shield \(\rightarrow\) Gown/Apron \(\rightarrow\) Mask/Respirator (perform hand hygiene after glove removal and final step).
* **ANTT Principles:** Identify Key Parts and Key Sites; do not touch contaminated surfaces or non-sterile items.
* **DUWL Maintenance:** Routine flushing and maintenance per manufacturer instructions and workplace policy; independent sterile water delivery systems required for surgical procedures involving open bone or mucosal reflection.
