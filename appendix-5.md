# Appendix 5 – IPC Incident Report Prompt Sheet

* **Date and Time of Incident:** Exact time occurrence took place.
* **Exact Location:** Specific room, chair, or reprocessing bay within the facility.
* **Personnel Involved:** Names of staff members, treating clinicians, and any patients affected.
* **Nature of Incident:** Objective description (e.g., percutaneous exposure via local anesthetic needle, blood splash to conjunctiva, sterilizer cycle parameter failure).
* **Immediate Actions Taken:** First aid administered, washing performed, supervisor notified, medical evaluation sought.
* **Equipment/Material Details:** Brand, model, batch numbers, or instrument types if relevant to equipment faults or exposure.
* **Preventative Recommendations:** Initial suggestions to prevent recurrence.
* **Signatures:** Signature and role of reporting staff member and reviewing supervisor.
